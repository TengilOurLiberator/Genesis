# Cue sheet: Genesis 1:1 to 2:3

One row per verse. The narration is read straight through in one recording, then cut into one clip per
verse. Each clip is played, then the world changes for as long as that verse needs before the next clip.
The player picks Adam or Eve before the darkness begins. Until verse 27 the player is a bodiless viewer
carried by the camera; at verse 27 they wake in the chosen body. They can look around from then on, and
are free to move once verse 2:3 ends.

| Verse | What is said | What happens in the pause | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1:1 | In the beginning | Nothing visible. Total black. | One very low, sustained tone fades in. |
| 1:2 | Without form, void; the Spirit on the waters | Still black. | Deep water moving below; a slow wind passes over you from behind. |
| 1:3 | Let there be light | A single point of light opens ahead and floods outward, revealing an endless dark sea. | Tone rises into a bright chord; first music. |
| 1:4 | Light divided from darkness | The light draws to one half of the sky, darkness to the other, with a clear edge between. | Chord splits into a high and a low voice. |
| 1:5 | Day and Night; the first day | The light fades to dusk, to black, then returns: the first evening and morning. No sun yet, the glow has no source. | Music settles; a soft bell marks the day. |
| 1:6 | A firmament in the midst of the waters | You are inside thick mist and water on every side. A clear space begins to open around you. | Muffled, underwater sound slowly clearing. |
| 1:7 | Waters under and waters above | The mist lifts into a cloud layer overhead; the sea settles flat below. | Rushing air upward, then open quiet. |
| 1:8 | The firmament called Heaven; the second day | The space between turns blue. Evening and morning. | Wind, high and clean; bell. |
| 1:9 | Let the dry land appear | The sea pulls back; bare stone and earth rise out of it, water pouring off. | Deep rumble, cascading water. |
| 1:10 | Earth and Seas; it was good | Camera rises to show coastlines, bays and mountains, still bare. | Surf on a shore for the first time. |
| 1:11 | Let the earth bring forth grass, herb, fruit tree | Green spreads over the bare ground from where you are, outward to the horizon. | Soft rustle growing; warm strings enter. |
| 1:12 | The earth brought forth | Trees push up and leaf out; flowers open; fruit appears. | Leaves, creaking wood. |
| 1:13 | The third day | Evening and morning over the new forests. | Bell. |
| 1:14 | Lights in the firmament, for signs and seasons | The sourceless glow dims; the sky waits, empty and dark blue. | Music thins to one held note. |
| 1:15 | To give light upon the earth | A glow gathers below the horizon in the east. | Note swells. |
| 1:16 | Two great lights, and the stars | The sun rises. It sets, and the moon rises. Then the stars come out, a few at first, then all of them. | Full theme for the sun; a quieter one for the moon; chimes for stars. |
| 1:17 | Set in the firmament | Sun and moon take up their paths across the sky. | Themes combine. |
| 1:18 | To rule the day and the night; it was good | One full day and night passes quickly, shadows sweeping across the land. | Steady pulse, like a slow clock. |
| 1:19 | The fourth day | Evening and morning, now with a real sunset and sunrise. | Bell. |
| 1:20 | Let the waters bring forth; let fowl fly | Camera goes down to the shore. The sea begins to stir with fish; the first birds lift off the water. | Splashing; first birdsong. |
| 1:21 | Great whales, every creature of the sea, every winged fowl | A whale surfaces offshore. Dolphins, turtles, squid; birds of many kinds overhead. | Whale call; wings. |
| 1:22 | Be fruitful and multiply | Schools thicken, flocks fill the sky. | Birdsong becomes a chorus. |
| 1:23 | The fifth day | Evening and morning with birds roosting and rising. | Bell. |
| 1:24 | Let the earth bring forth cattle, creeping things, beasts | Animals emerge from the ground and the forest edge. | Hooves, lowing, calls. |
| 1:25 | God made the beasts; it was good | Herds move across the plains; small creatures in the grass. | Full daytime ambience. |
| 1:26 | Let us make man in our image | Everything hushes. The camera descends into a clearing. Light gathers on the ground in two places. | Animals fall silent; music drops to a single voice. |
| 1:27 | Male and female created he them | Two figures of light take shape. The view moves into the chosen one. You open your eyes and have a body; the other stands beside you. | A breath in. Heartbeat. Then the music returns, warm. |
| 1:28 | Blessed; be fruitful; have dominion | Animals approach without fear. Your companion looks at you, then at them. | Gentle theme; animals close by. |
| 1:29 | Every herb and fruit tree given for food | Fruit trees around the clearing catch the light. | Bright, light motif. |
| 1:30 | To every beast, every green herb | The animals begin to graze. | Calm ambience. |
| 1:31 | Very good; the sixth day | A long golden sunset over everything that was made. | Full theme, all parts together; bell. |
| 2:1 | The heavens and the earth were finished | Night. The whole sky of stars. | Music thins out. |
| 2:2 | On the seventh day God rested | Dawn. Nothing new appears. Everything is still. | Near silence; a few birds. |
| 2:3 | God blessed the seventh day | Light holds on the clearing. The narration ends. You can move. Your companion turns to you and speaks first. | Final chord fades; the world's own sounds remain. |
